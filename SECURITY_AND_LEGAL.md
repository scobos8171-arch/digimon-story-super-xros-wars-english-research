# Security and legal boundaries

Do not commit or attach:

- Nintendo DS ROM images;
- DeSmuME `.dst` or battery `.dsv` files;
- BIOS, firmware, keys, or emulator bundles;
- complete proprietary archives or bulk extracted sprites/audio/video;
- personal tokens, credentials, absolute user paths, or private logs.

Binary releases must be patches that require the user to supply the verified
clean game. Patchers must validate the input SHA-256 before writing output.

Small screenshots and minimal excerpts may be used only where reasonably needed
for research, documentation, and issue identification. Prefer hashes, indices,
offsets, and independently authored diagrams wherever possible.
